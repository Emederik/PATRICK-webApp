## 🇫🇷 Version française

Je ne suis pas développeuse.

## 🇬🇧 English version

I'm not a developer.
This week, I still shipped a real AI assistant. 🍁

Meet **Patrick** — a bilingual (FR/EN) guide for young Working Holiday travellers settling in Montreal. Free, no login: SIN, health coverage, leases, banking, winter, scams, budget… It answers **from verified official sources**, and **cites them**.

What surprised me most was how much I could build myself, from scratch:
• a corpus of fiches, each fact-checked,
• a real **RAG on AWS Bedrock** (Knowledge Base + S3 Vectors + Claude Haiku),
• deployed on **Vercel**, code on **GitHub**,
• with real guardrails: no personal data, capped costs, and — crucially — **it asks for YOUR nationality** (the program covers 36 countries, not just one).

15 years in global mobility + prompt engineering + the right tools = **a real product, not a mockup**.

**Me :** Can I rent without a Canadian job?

**Patrick :** # Can I rent without a Canadian job?

**Short answer: Yes, absolutely.** Landlords and agents care about your **ability to pay rent**, not your employment status. Here's what matters:

## What landlords actually check

1. **Proof of funds** (most important)
   - Bank statements showing you have money
   - Proof of savings or financial support
   - A guarantor (someone who co-signs and backs you financially)

2. **Credit history** (if available)
   - If you're new to Canada, you likely won't have a Canadian credit file yet — that's normal and expected
   - Landlords understand this for newcomers

3. **References**
   - Previous landlord references (from your home country, if applicable)
   - Personal or professional references

4. **Your passport & valid ID**
   - Proof you're legally in Canada (your work permit, study permit, or PR status)

## Practical tips

- **Get a guarantor if possible** — a friend, family member, or someone already established in Canada who's willing to co-sign. This strengthens your application significantly.
- **Show your bank statements** — if you have 3–6 months of rent saved, you're in a strong position.
- **Use an agent** — they work with landlords regularly and understand newcomer situations. Normally, the **landlord pays the agent's commission**, not you.
- **Don't contact the agent listed on an ad** — that's the landlord's agent. Find your own agent instead; they have access to all listings in the province.

## Housing near metro (the smart move)

**Absolutely aim for 15 min walk max to a metro station.** Here's why:
- **Winter is brutal** — waiting for a bus in -20°C is rough, and buses run late
- If you're far from metro, you risk **staying home all winter**
- The metro is reliable year-round

At **$1,500/month**, you're in a realistic range for a shared apartment or small bachelor in accessible neighborhoods (Plateau, Rosemont, Villeray, etc.).

**Pro tip:** Look into **cession de bail** (taking over someone's lease) — often cheaper and faster than signing a new one.
